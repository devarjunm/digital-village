1. Introduction
India's villages contribute significantly to agriculture and the economy. Over the past decade, the Government of India and the Government of Maharashtra have introduced numerous digital platforms such as MahaDBT, MahaVistaar, e-Peek Pahani, Digital Crop Survey, CropSAP, MahaKrushi, AgriStack Farmer ID, PM-Kisan, and APMC e-Mandi.
While these applications solve specific problems, farmers often need to install and learn multiple apps to access different services. This fragmentation creates confusion, duplication of effort, and a poor user experience.
Digital Village aims to address this challenge by providing a unified digital platform that integrates essential farmer-centric services into a single application.

2. Vision Statement
"To build India's most trusted digital ecosystem that empowers every village by providing accessible, intelligent, and integrated digital services, enabling farmers and rural citizens to make informed decisions, improve productivity, and enhance their quality of life."
________________________________________
Expanded Vision
Digital Village is envisioned as more than just a mobile application.
It aims to become:
•	A digital identity platform for rural citizens. 
•	An agricultural intelligence platform. 
•	A rural governance platform. 
•	A knowledge-sharing community. 
•	A marketplace connecting farmers directly with buyers. 
•	A digital assistant for every farmer. 
The long-term goal is to create a platform where a farmer can access all essential digital services without switching between multiple government applications.
