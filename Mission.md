Mission Statement
"To simplify rural digital services by building a unified, multilingual, AI-enabled platform that connects farmers with government schemes, agricultural advisory, market intelligence, weather information, and community support through a single mobile application."
________________________________________
Mission Objectives
Objective 1
Reduce the complexity of accessing government services.
Instead of requiring multiple applications, Digital Village should provide one platform where users can discover services and, where permitted, connect to official systems.
________________________________________
Objective 2
Provide intelligent recommendations.
Rather than asking farmers to search through hundreds of government schemes, the application should recommend schemes based on:
•	Landholding 
•	Crop type 
•	District 
•	Category 
•	Irrigation status 
•	Farmer profile 
•	Other eligibility information (subject to available data and official criteria) 
________________________________________
Objective 3
Improve agricultural productivity.
Provide AI-assisted crop guidance using images and contextual information.
Possible capabilities include:
•	Crop identification 
•	Pest detection 
•	Disease detection 
•	Nutrient deficiency guidance 
•	Best practice recommendations 
The application should clearly indicate when advice is informational and encourage consultation with agricultural.
________________________________________
Objective 4
Improve market awareness.
Provide market price information for nearby APMC markets and other available sources so farmers can compare prices before deciding where to sell.
________________________________________
Objective 5
Improve weather preparedness.
Provide weather forecasts, rainfall predictions, and agricultural advisories based on available weather data.
________________________________________
Objective 6
Create a farmer community.
Allow verified farmers to:
•	Ask questions 
•	Share experiences 
•	Upload photos 
•	Discuss crop issues 
•	Learn from agricultural experts

Problem Background
Currently, farmers interact with multiple digital systems.
Examples include:
•	MahaDBT 
•	MahaVistaar 
•	e-Peek Pahani 
•	Digital Crop Survey 
•	CropSAP 
•	MahaKrushi 
•	PM-Kisan 
•	AgriStack Farmer ID 
Each serves a different purpose.
As a result:
•	Users need multiple applications. 
•	Authentication is repeated. 
•	Information is scattered. 
•	User experience is inconsistent. 
Digital Village aims to simplify the experience by serving as a unified platform. Where direct integration with official government systems is not available, the application can provide guidance, links, or complementary services rather than duplicating official records.
